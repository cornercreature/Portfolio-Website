Nicole is a designer with a wide range of skills from illustration to coding, who enjoys experimenting with different mediums and approaches. Right now, she is focused on pushing the conventions and standardized assumptions of digital and print design. Moving forward, she would like to do more with branding and motion. As a speaker of three languages, she holds a forever fascination for multilingual design contexts. She is currently pursuing her BFA in Graphic Design and Computation at the Rhode Island School of Design.
